Your goal is one short post on your profile, ready to share. We'll use **Primal on your phone**. You can use the same Nostr identity in another app later. Button labels may differ by version.

<span id="step-2"></span>
## 1. Install the app


Open the [official Primal download page](https://primal.net/download) on your phone. Choose the iPhone or Android version and install it from the store linked there. Open the app.

**Check:** You should see a way to create an identity or sign in with one you already have.

## 2. Set up your identity

- **New to Nostr?** Create an identity in the app. Before going further, use the app's backup option to save your private key securely. If you need help, read <a href={guidePath('keys-and-security', 'en')}>Keys and Security</a>.
- **Already have Nostr keys?** Sign in with your existing identity using a method the app supports. Do not create a second identity just to follow this guide.

Your **npub** is public and safe to share. Your **nsec** is private: never put it in a post, send it to someone, or type it into a website. Keep a backup you can access if you lose your phone.

**Check:** The app shows your profile or feed under your chosen identity. You have saved the private key before publishing.

## 3. Give people a reason to recognise you

Open your profile and add a name or nickname and one sentence about an interest or why you joined. For example: “I enjoy books and discovering new places.” A profile photo can wait.

**Check:** Your name and short description appear when you return to your profile.

## 4. Publish a first post

Find the app's action for writing a new post. Start with plain text. You can adapt this example:

> Hello Nostr. I'm new here and looking for book recommendations. What have you enjoyed reading lately?

Write something true about why you're here, then publish. You do not need a perfect introduction, a photo, or hashtags to begin.

**Check:** The post appears on your profile. If it does not, wait a moment and refresh once.

## 5. Check that your post is visible

Open the post from your profile. If the app offers a share action, copy its link and open it in a browser or send it to someone you trust. Seeing it outside your own feed is a stronger check that it reached other readers.

If the post is missing or the link does not open, check your connection and try again after a short wait. Posts travel through relays, which may delay or reject them. Do not create a new identity or publish the same post repeatedly while checking. The <a href={guidePath('troubleshooting', 'en')}>troubleshooting guide</a> covers posts that do not appear.

**You are done when your post is visible on your profile and you have checked its shared link, if available.** Next, find a few people who interest you in <a href={guidePath('finding-community', 'en')}>Finding Your Community</a>.
